Weekly summary email

Everyone gets one summary a week: their open To-Dos, Rocks that are off track and scorecard numbers that missed their goal. Team admins and viewers also get a short summary of their team. Nobody is emailed when there is nothing to report. Each person can turn it off for themselves.

Calendar links in invitations

Meeting emails always attach a calendar file that works in Outlook, Google Calendar and Apple Calendar, with no outside connection.
